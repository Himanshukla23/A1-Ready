# A1 ReadyCheck — Problem Statement

## 1. Product Overview
A1 ReadyCheck is an AI-assisted German A1 exam readiness platform. It helps learners understand their preparation level before booking Goethe or ÖSD A1 examinations.

## 2. Problem Statement
Many learners complete A1 German courses and book examinations without objectively understanding their readiness. They may have gaps in vocabulary, grammar or reading comprehension. These gaps can lead to exam failure, financial loss and wasted preparation time. Learners also lack clear guidance about what to practise next.

## 3. Proposed Solution
Build a web application that combines a structured 12-question diagnostic assessment with an AI learning assistant named Doro.

## 4. Core Assessment
- 12 original A1-level questions.
- 4 vocabulary questions.
- 4 grammar questions.
- 4 reading comprehension questions.
- Four multiple-choice options per question.
- Deterministic answer evaluation and scoring.
- Overall score and skill-wise breakdown.
- Readiness category and actionable recommendations.

## 5. Doro AI Assistant
Doro is a personal A1 German learning companion. It can explain German concepts, answer A1 learning questions, explain submitted assessment mistakes and recommend personalised practice. During an active assessment, it may explain general concepts but must not reveal answers to active questions. Doro must never calculate, modify or override assessment scores.

## 6. Target Users
Learners who have completed A1 German and are preparing for Goethe or ÖSD A1 examinations.

## 7. Core User Needs
- Understand current preparation level.
- Identify vocabulary, grammar and reading weaknesses.
- Understand mistakes.
- Receive personalised learning guidance.
- Make better-informed examination booking decisions.

## 8. Product Principles
- Accuracy over impressive-looking scores.
- Honest and transparent readiness feedback.
- AI assists learning; deterministic logic evaluates answers.
- Simple, accessible and mobile-responsive experience.
- No guarantee of official examination success.

## 9. MVP Scope
Landing page, assessment introduction, 12-question assessment, answer navigation, scoring engine, results dashboard, skill breakdown, personalised recommendations, Doro assistant, and assessment retake.

## 10. Out of Scope
User authentication, payments, official exam certification, full Goethe/ÖSD simulation, listening and speaking assessments, and complex learner analytics.

## 11. Product Outcome
Help learners move from uncertainty about their A1 preparation to a clearer understanding of their strengths, weaknesses and next learning steps.
